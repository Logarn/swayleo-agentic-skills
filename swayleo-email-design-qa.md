# swayleo-email-design-qa

Establish brand references, approved copy, channel and target clients. Define hierarchy, imagery, mobile layout, CTA treatment and accessible contrast. Keep critical meaning in live text; provide alt text guidance. Inspect actual renders when available for clipping, readability, links, personalization fallbacks, image loading and mobile behavior. Separate observed defects from untested concerns. Use the connected design or ESP tools available; do not imply Figma, Customer.io or Klaviyo access exists if it does not. Deliver the brief or prioritized QA findings with evidence.
