# swayleo-campaign-calendar

Use the brand brief, commercial dates, product availability, existing flows and verified segments. For each campaign include date, timezone, objective, angle, audience definition, exclusions, channel, owner, production deadline and approval status. If audience criteria are unknown, flag them; do not select a segment solely by name. Check frequency and conflicts with flows and other campaigns. Label new segmentation ideas for review. Preserve client deadlines and distinguish planned campaigns from scheduled sends. Do not schedule or send unless asked.
