# Campaign Calendar

## Purpose and provenance

Expanded using the agenda and recovered cross-brand calendar/copy workflows.

This workflow applies across brands by substituting the current brand's sources, account, scope and approval rules. It is a reusable instruction set, not evidence that a live system is configured.

## Inputs

Completed intake, approved offer terms, audience definitions, products, commercial dates, send timezone, previous calendar, flow overlap and ownership.

If an input is unavailable, identify its effect and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not fill factual gaps with another brand's assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Read the current intake and the latest explicit client corrections. Build an offer ledger with source, date and approval status; a newer proposed draft does not overrule a client decision.
2. Give each campaign one job and a distinct angle. Do not repeat the discount as the entire reason for every email.
3. Record send date, local time and timezone. Separate offer expiry from send time, early-access expiry and shipping cutoff. Resolve daylight-saving changes when converting actual dates.
4. Specify the audience by definition or verified segment ID plus exclusions. If the brief says members, confirm what member means and which purchase destination applies.
5. Map product, offer code, mechanics, landing destination, primary objective and supporting content. Flag unavailable assets, ambiguous terms and unconfirmed inventory.
6. Include production and approval dependencies. A planned send is not a scheduled campaign. Do not release work merely because a date appears on the calendar.
7. Mirror the calendar's campaign IDs, angles and terms in the copy-doc strategy. Preserve existing copy; leave new writing cells blank when the assignment is strategy only.
8. Recheck the month as a sequence for repetition, audience pressure, flow collisions and missing events. Publish only to the requested month/tab.

## Deliverable

Campaign ID | Date/time/zone | Channel | Job | Angle | Audience/exclusions | Product | Offer/expiry | CTA | Owner | Dependencies | Approval state. Add a short monthly strategy summary.

Use the output worksheet inside the downloadable library ZIP to structure a working draft. Adapt it to the requested deliverable; do not create extra files unless useful.

## Example

Early access can end while the member price continues. Keep those as different facts in the calendar and brief.

## Verification and completion

Every campaign has an audience and job; offer mechanics are sourced; calendar and brief agree; unrelated months remain intact; proposals remain labelled.

Report what was completed, the source or saved record that proves it, and any unresolved blocker. Distinguish a draft, a saved change and a live action. Publishing, posting, scheduling or changing live systems requires the corresponding user request and available tools.
