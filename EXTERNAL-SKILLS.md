# External skills: source and setup guide

Selected from public skill directories and primary author repositories on October 29, 2026. Sources are pinned to commits. Full folders and licence files are in the library ZIP; the links below open readable previews in this repo.

**48 added skills from 7 source repositories.** These are external instructions, separate from the local Codex inventory and the 23 Swayleo workflows. They have not all been tested with live accounts.

## Start with the job

- Direct-response copy: Rob Palmer direct-response-copy, then copychief. Use compliance-checker only as a review aid.
- Lifecycle strategy: Corey emails + SMS; Rebecca customer-journey-mapping and retention-churn-prevention.
- Klaviyo: klaviyo-analyst for strategy/audits; klaviyo-developer for implementation; klaviyo-django for personalization syntax.
- Email design: Kinga Dow for brand-component design; Email Love for its exportable email structure, repair, migration and quality gates. These are different design-system paths.
- Marketing and sales: product-marketing, customer-research, offers, marketing-plan, revops and sales-enablement.
- Project management: technical-project-management, technical-program-management, kanban-guru and meetings.

## Choose a source, not every competing workflow at once

Keep Swayleo brand and approval rules as the task context. Select the external workflow that fits the job; do not load every copywriting or design skill into one task. Cadence, percentage thresholds, attribution defaults and sample claims are not universal brand rules. A live send, activation, account connection or paid conversion service needs its own task authorization.
