# Academy Resource

## Purpose and provenance

Expanded from the academy agenda; no existing academy inventory is assumed.

This workflow applies across brands by substituting the current brand's sources, account, scope and approval rules. It is a reusable instruction set, not evidence that a live system is configured.

## Inputs

Existing academy index, team learning needs, source links, internal SOPs or walkthroughs and intended audience.

If an input is unavailable, identify its effect and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not fill factual gaps with another brand's assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Inventory what actually exists before describing academy coverage. Suggested providers are not confirmed contents.
2. Record title, provider, topic, level, access requirements, date checked and the job the resource helps someone perform.
3. Distinguish freely accessible from openly licensed. Link to a course/article/video unless copying or adaptation is permitted.
4. Prioritize internal resources around repeated work: onboarding, briefs, copy review, audience checks, design QA, handoffs and automation troubleshooting.
5. Convert a verified workflow into a short lesson: purpose, inputs, demonstration, practice task, expected output and review criteria.
6. Use synthetic or properly authorized examples. Remove client identifiers, private links, prices and operational details that are not approved for the training audience.
7. Add a maintenance owner and review trigger. A platform-specific walkthrough becomes stale when its UI/API changes; date it and verify before reusing.

## Deliverable

Resource register and prioritized lesson backlog. Internal lesson: objective | prerequisites | walkthrough | exercise | expected result | review criteria | owner.

Use the output worksheet inside the downloadable library ZIP to structure a working draft. Adapt it to the requested deliverable; do not create extra files unless useful.

## Example

A lesson on campaign QA can use a fictional kit-image mismatch and show how to compare source, copy, design and destination.

## Verification and completion

Coverage is verified; permissions are respected; each resource has a practical use; examples contain no private client data; stale resources are labelled.

Report what was completed, the source or saved record that proves it, and any unresolved blocker. Distinguish a draft, a saved change and a live action. Publishing, posting, scheduling or changing live systems requires the corresponding user request and available tools.
