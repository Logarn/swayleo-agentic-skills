# swayleo-lifecycle-copy

Use the client's voice, offer terms, audience, lifecycle stage and desired action. If essential facts are missing, flag them rather than inventing claims, discounts or urgency. Supply channel-appropriate copy with a clear hierarchy and CTA. For email include subject, preview, body and CTA; for SMS keep the message concise and flag required opt-out and locale review. Preserve variable syntax and identify fallback needs. Explain meaningful alternatives briefly. Check consistency with the brief and destination. Drafting does not authorize sending.
