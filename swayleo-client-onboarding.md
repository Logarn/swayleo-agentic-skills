# Client Onboarding

## Purpose and provenance

New cross-brand workflow joining recovered ownership and document practices.

This workflow applies across brands by substituting the current brand's sources, account, scope and approval rules. It is a reusable instruction set, not evidence that a live system is configured.

## Inputs

Signed scope or confirmed services, intake, brand/product resources, contacts, account ownership, current calendars/flows and access requirements.

If an input is unavailable, identify its effect and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not fill factual gaps with another brand's assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Confirm scope, deliverables and exclusions. A missing agency build assignment is not permission to take over client-owned activation.
2. Create a resource and ownership map: account manager, strategy, copy, design, build, QA and approval roles. Verify specific batch ownership when the general assignment sheet is unresolved.
3. Inventory the four core documents and current master readiness. Use client-folder for setup rather than duplicating documents unnecessarily.
4. Check service access by actual account and capability: read, edit, review, build and send can differ. Do not infer permissions from a recent-account list.
5. Establish review route, source of truth, naming convention and timing requirements. Unagreed standards stay open decisions.
6. Audit current audiences, flows, campaigns and business priorities at the level needed for the first deliverable. Do not start a broad migration without scope.
7. Produce a first-work plan with dependencies and decision owners. Reuse existing tasks and preserve historical commitments.
8. Track readiness separately from completion; access and client input can block delivery even when an internal template is ready.

## Deliverable

Scope/ownership matrix; resources and access register; first-deliverable plan; blockers/decisions; next review checkpoint.

Use the output worksheet inside the downloadable library ZIP to structure a working draft. Adapt it to the requested deliverable; do not create extra files unless useful.

## Example

The design agency is confirmed, but its tracker is read-only. Record the owner and access blocker instead of assigning a different designer by assumption.

## Verification and completion

Scope verified; owners explicit; resources canonical; access tested; readiness not overstated; no duplicate task/doc creation.

Report what was completed, the source or saved record that proves it, and any unresolved blocker. Distinguish a draft, a saved change and a live action. Publishing, posting, scheduling or changing live systems requires the corresponding user request and available tools.
