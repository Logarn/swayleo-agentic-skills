# Brand Context

## Purpose and provenance

New reusable entrypoint derived from the recovered cross-brand review process.

This workflow applies across brands by substituting the current brand's sources, account, scope and approval rules. It is a reusable instruction set, not evidence that a live system is configured.

## Inputs

Brand brief, current intake, product sheets, policy/offer sources, client decisions, canonical docs and requested task.

If an input is unavailable, identify its effect and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not fill factual gaps with another brand's assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the correct brand, account and month. Similar product names and repeated templates do not establish identity.
2. Build a source register: source type, date, authority, exact fact and link. Separate product evidence, client instruction, internal proposal and current live setup.
3. Capture audience, voice, products, permitted claims, offer mechanics, guarantee/shipping exclusions, CTA routes and approval owners.
4. Apply later explicit corrections to the same fact while retaining history. Do not assume the newest file is the most authoritative or that an unconfirmed offer was cancelled.
5. List conflicts and missing inputs with their practical effect. Continue independent work that does not depend on an answer.
6. For another brand, rebuild the facts. Reuse the framework, never client-specific claims, offer codes, subscription terms or permissions.

## Deliverable

One-page working brief; source/decision ledger; unresolved facts; canonical-link register; task-specific constraints.

Use the output worksheet inside the downloadable library ZIP to structure a working draft. Adapt it to the requested deliverable; do not create extra files unless useful.

## Example

A current client comment corrects the free gift while the older calendar still says BOGO. Preserve the distinction and flag the inconsistent documents.

## Verification and completion

Every material claim has a source; proposals and approvals are distinct; identity/account/month match; brand-specific assumptions are not imported.

Report what was completed, the source or saved record that proves it, and any unresolved blocker. Distinguish a draft, a saved change and a live action. Publishing, posting, scheduling or changing live systems requires the corresponding user request and available tools.
