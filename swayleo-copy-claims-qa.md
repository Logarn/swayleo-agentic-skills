# Copy Claims Qa

## Purpose and provenance

Recovered from cross-brand November copy reviews and saved QA corrections.

This workflow applies across brands by substituting the current brand's sources, account, scope and approval rules. It is a reusable instruction set, not evidence that a live system is configured.

## Inputs

Current copy, latest comments, approved product sheets, offer terms, policy pages, calendar and destination references.

If an input is unavailable, identify its effect and continue work that does not depend on it. Do not fill factual gaps with another brand's assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Establish the current review copy and relevant comments. Review against the latest authoritative correction, not an obsolete calendar or an internal done note.
2. Check product identity, SKU/size, kit contents, pack quantity, use case and instructions. Never infer use frequency from a catchy line.
3. Match each measurable claim to the right evidence and footnote: product, population, endpoint, timeframe and qualification. Flag a mismatched claim/footnote pair rather than inventing a replacement statistic.
4. Verify offer mechanics: qualifying product, discount, gift, quantity, code, eligibility, expiry and exclusions. Separate free-gift wording from BOGO and early-access timing from price expiry.
5. Review guarantee language and exclusions. Do not call an offer completely risk-free if shipping or return costs are excluded.
6. Confirm proof is attributed to the product actually reviewed. Fixed testimonials or alternatives must not imply they describe every dynamic cart item.
7. Review subject, preview, body, buttons, SMS and disclaimers together for contradictory statements.
8. Return a short correction list with the actual wording, evidence and requested change. Separate confirmed defects from questions needing the client. Preserve pending approval state.

## Deliverable

Location | Current wording | Issue | Evidence | Proposed change/question | Owner | Status. Include unresolved facts that block design or send readiness.

Use the output worksheet inside the downloadable library ZIP to structure a working draft. Adapt it to the requested deliverable; do not create extra files unless useful.

## Example

“Early access ends today” does not imply the member price ends today. “Two packs” does not establish whether they are separate products or one combined pack.

## Verification and completion

All material claims/terms are sourced; exact products and timeframes match; channels agree; proposed replacements are not falsely labelled approved.

Report what was completed, the source or saved record that proves it, and any unresolved blocker. Distinguish a draft, a saved change and a live action. Publishing, posting, scheduling or changing live systems requires the corresponding user request and available tools.
